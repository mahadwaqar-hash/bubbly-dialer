Android Call Interceptor Setup

How to make this app intercept Google Maps "Call" clicks

How Call Interception Works on Android:

When you tap "Call" on a business card in Google Maps or Chrome, Android broadcasts an Intent with the tel: URI scheme. By registering an intent filter in your app's AndroidManifest.xml, Android will show BubblyDialer in the "Open with / Complete action using" sheet!

1. `AndroidManifest.xml` Intent Filter Configuration:

Add this inside your main <activity> in React Native, Flutter, or Capacitor:

<!-- Intercept standard dialer tel: links from Google Maps & Chrome -->
<intent-filter>
    <action android:name="android.intent.action.DIAL" />
    <action android:name="android.intent.action.VIEW" />
    <category android:name="android.intent.category.DEFAULT" />
    <category android:name="android.intent.category.BROWSABLE" />
    <data android:scheme="tel" />
</intent-filter>

<!-- Intercept "Share from Google Maps" to capture business place link -->
<intent-filter>
    <action android:name="android.intent.action.SEND" />
    <category android:name="android.intent.category.DEFAULT" />
    <data android:mimeType="text/plain" />
</intent-filter>
2. Required Permissions:
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.CALL_PHONE" />
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW" />
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET" />
3. Automated Google Places Reverse Lookup:

When the phone number arrives in the intent, call Google Places Find Place API:

https://maps.googleapis.com/maps/api/place/findplacefromtext/json
  ?input=+14155552671
  &inputtype=phonenumber
  &fields=name,rating,user_ratings_total,website,types,formatted_phone_number
  &key=YOUR_API_KEY
